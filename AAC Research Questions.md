Initial Questions

\*Individuals and families mentioned in this questionnaire will be provided with appropriate pseudonyms. All information given is voluntary and can be revoked or removed at any time prior to the research’s finalization. All questions listed are voluntary, and can be skipped if the responder deems fit\*

1. Please describe your role in disability advocacy and the use of AAC devices.  
     
2. Have you previously been included in any research surrounding disability advocacy and/or the use of AAC devices?  
3. What population (age, disability type) of children have you supported to use AAC?    
     
4. What are the most common misconceptions you have seen about AAC devices and their usage?  
     
5. What are the most common barriers you have seen in regard to AAC devices?  
     
6. What are the most common benefits you have seen in regard to AAC devices?  
     
7. Do you have any experience using SDG’s (speech generating devices)? If so, please share the types/versions/brands you have used.  
     
8. Please share any non-SDG AAC devices you have used/supported individuals using.  
     
9. Do you have any experience using/supporting AAC device users that are multilingual?  
     
10. Have you seen any advantages and/or disadvantages in multilingual AAC devices?  
      
11.  From your personal experience, what have you found beneficial in the design of multilingual AAC devices?  
       
12.  From your personal experience, what design aspects could be beneficial in multilingual AAC devices?  
13. Is there anything else that you would like to share about your experiences supporting individuals to use AAC, specifically multilingual users that could support the development of a multilingual application for an AAC device?  
      
14. Feel free to answer anything else.
